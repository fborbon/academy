What you need

  • Python: none for the Intro module beyond having written a few lines of code; each module builds on the previous one.
  • Maths: school level. The Advanced notebooks explain the science they use as they go.
  • Hardware: any laptop from the last decade. Every notebook runs on a CPU in minutes; two notebooks download a small dataset once. Notebook 03's PySpark section needs Java 17+.

Data and credits

  • Most data is generated in the notebooks or ships with the libraries (scikit-learn, statsmodels, lifelines).
  • Ames Housing data: De Cock, D. (2011), Journal of Statistics Education 19(3), via OpenML (public).
  • GBSG2 breast-cancer trial: Schumacher et al. (1994), Journal of Clinical Oncology, as distributed with lifelines.
  • US macroeconomic data: as distributed with statsmodels (macrodata).
  • Bike Sharing data: Fanaee-T, H. and Gama, J. (2013), Progress in Artificial Intelligence, UCI repository (CC BY 4.0), via OpenML.
  • Planetary orbital elements: standard published values (NASA planetary fact sheets).
